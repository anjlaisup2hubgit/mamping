Mamping screen tour

Every screen and popup in order, at phone size (390 × 844). The gray circle marks the button pressed next. Under each picture: how you got there. Captured on 52 steps with a local copy of the app; nothing was sent to OpenStreetMap.

Not shown or not pressed

  • Log in opens the real OpenStreetMap sign-in page, so it was not pressed. The signed-in pictures use a demo account saved in the browser.
  • Anything that sends was not finished: Send to OpenStreetMap (signed out, so the app refuses), Save to OpenStreetMap in the building panel, Upload now, Add details to OpenStreetMap, Share outline with Mamping, Send for training, AI scan on the tool rail, Retry, Already on map, Retry account sync, Undo in My edits, Train AI and Log out.
  • Browser “are you sure?” boxes (Teach the AI, Retry on an interrupted edit, Already on map, Assign or Import older drafts, Recover old labels) are drawn by the browser and do not show in screenshots.
  • Drive results needs a real drive with pictures taken every 15 m, so it was not reached.
  • Training labels needs pictures on the training server; the page is shown empty.
  • Single shape tools (Tidy all, Square, Line up, Street, Snap, Turn, Copy, Undo/Redo, dragging corners) are on the tool rail in the pictures but were not pressed one by one.
  • The video AI call was blocked, so the review page shows “AI is unavailable. Using your location instead.”
